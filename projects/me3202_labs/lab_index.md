# ME3202 lab index

**Gajendra | 2301ME18 | IIT Patna**

These objectives are summarized from the supplied older ME312-labelled manuals for Gajendra's ME3202 course and interview preparation. This index does not reproduce a senior's submitted results.

| Lab | Topic | What to explain in an interview |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Cantilever-beam damping by logarithmic decrement | How successive peaks estimate damping ratio, and how an accelerometer measures response. |
| 2 | Cantilever-beam natural frequencies by resonance | Why response peaks near a mode's natural frequency and how added mass can affect measurement. |
| 3 | MATLAB signal spectrum and power spectrum | Sampling rate, frequency resolution, DFT/FFT, and why aliasing or leakage can mislead. |
| 4 | Positive and negative feedback on a cantilever beam | How displacement and velocity feedback change effective stiffness or damping. |
| 5 | Free and forced vibration of a single-degree-of-freedom system | Underdamped response, transmissibility, and the effect of damping. |
| 6 | Arduino Uno keypad and I2C LCD | Pin mapping, I2C wiring, serial debugging, and checking each key. |
| 7 | 2-DOF planar manipulator dynamics and PID | Joint position error, torque, gravity, overshoot, and tuning gains. |
| 8 | DC motor speed control with PWM and Simulink PID | Open-loop versus closed-loop speed, motor driver, power supply, and feedback. |
| 9 | Transfer function and root-locus analysis | Closed-loop poles, stability, damping type, and how gain changes the response. |
